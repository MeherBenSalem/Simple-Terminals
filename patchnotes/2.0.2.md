# OmniVault 2.0.2

## Fixed (Minecraft 26.3)

- **Terminal clicks.** In the Storage and Crafting Terminals, left-click took only half a stack, right-click did
  nothing, shift-click took a single item instead of moving the stack to your inventory, and left-clicking with an
  item on the cursor inserted one item instead of the whole stack. All four work as intended again.
- The same mix-up affected the lock button on drawers, the Trash and Storage screens and the backpack buttons,
  scrollbar and filter slots. They respond to the right mouse buttons again.

Minecraft 26.3 numbers mouse buttons differently from earlier versions (left is 1, right is 3), and those
screens read the raw number. They now translate it the same way Minecraft's own inventory screens do.
1.20.1, 1.21.1 and 26.2 are unchanged apart from the version number.
