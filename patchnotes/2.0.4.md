# OmniVault 2.0.4

## Fixed (every version)

- **See-through world beside terminals.** Next to a Storage or Crafting Terminal, parts of the surrounding blocks
  could disappear, so you looked straight through into caves or the sky. The terminals' screen bezel is set back
  and leaves small openings at the sides, yet the block told Minecraft it was a solid cube, so the blocks around it
  skipped drawing the faces that touch it. Terminals now let their neighbours draw those faces, as the Nexus Core
  already does.
- **Bus connectors from the side.** A cable attached to the side of an Import, Export or Storage Bus (or a
  Destructor or Constructor) connected, but its end floated in mid-air beside the bus, because the bus only had a
  connector at its back. Those blocks now draw a connector on every side where a cable or another network block
  joins. Buses built before this update get their connectors the next time their network forms, such as when the
  world loads.
