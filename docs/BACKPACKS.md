## Using a backpack

- **Right-click** it in your hand to open it, or press **B** to open the one you wear (or the first one in your inventory).
- **Wear it** in the chest armour slot; it shows on your back.
- **Sneak-right-click** a block face to put it down as a block; right-click the placed backpack to open it and break it to
  pick it up again. The placed backpack works with hoppers and pipes.
- **Sort** compacts and orders the contents; large backpacks scroll.
- Backpacks can't go inside backpacks (or shulker boxes, or other storage items).

## Upgrading

Leather becomes Copper, Iron, Gold and then Diamond by surrounding it with eight of the next material in a crafting
grid; Diamond becomes Netherite at a smithing table. The contents and upgrades always come along.

## Upgrades

Install upgrade cards in the upgrade slots on the left of the backpack screen. Each one has a tab on the side with a
9-slot filter (allow or deny list, optional exact data matching) and an on/off switch.

| Upgrade | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Pickup | Items you pick up that pass the filter go straight into the backpack. |
| Magnet | Pulls nearby items (and experience) to you. Range adjustable (up to 8 blocks). |
| Void | Destroys filtered items when you pick them up, or only the excess the backpack can't hold. |
| Feeding | Eats food from the backpack when you're hungry, skipping harmful food. |
| Restock | Tops up your hotbar stacks from the backpack. |
| Compacting | Turns nine (or four) of an item into its block, using only recipes that can be reversed. |
| Crafting | Adds a 3×3 crafting grid tab. |
| Everlasting | A dropped backpack never despawns and can't be destroyed. |
| Deposit | Sneak-right-click a chest (or any container) with the backpack to empty matching items into it. |

Placed backpacks keep running their Magnet, Pickup, Void and Compacting upgrades.

## Where the contents live

A backpack's contents are stored in the world save under the backpack's own ID, not on the item, so a full Netherite
backpack never bloats your inventory data or network traffic. In creative, copying a backpack makes a second item that
opens the same inventory, just like copying a shulker box.
